01 / About

Curiosity has been the common thread.

From understanding how things work to finding ways to make them work better.

I began my career at Ventes Avenues, helping brands tap into high-impact media opportunities. At DDB Mudra, I got hands-on with campaign execution and media planning—managing over ₹2.5 Cr in quarterly budgets and collaborating with brand managers to design and optimize campaigns across Meta, YouTube, and programmatic platforms. These experiences grounded me in fast-paced brand environments and taught me to balance creativity with outcomes.

To deepen my understanding of how businesses grow and compete, I pursued a PGP in Technology & Business Management at Masters’ Union, which eventually pushed me toward product thinking. That’s what brought me to Aditya Birla Capital, where the work is tangible: real investors, real transactions, real consequences if something breaks.

I’m a generalist by background and a product thinker by inclination. The problems I find most interesting are the ones that sit at the edge of what a user needs and what a system can actually do.
