01 / About

Curiosity is the through line.

From studying engineering to working in digital ad sales, leading media campaigns and now building digital products for investors, the context has changed. The instinct to understand how things work—and make them work better—hasn't.

I began my career at Ventes Avenues, helping brands tap into high-impact media opportunities. At DDB Mudra, I moved closer to campaign execution and media planning—managing quarterly budgets of more than ₹2.5 crore and working with brand teams to design and optimise campaigns across Meta, YouTube and programmatic platforms.

To deepen my understanding of how businesses grow and compete, I pursued a PGP in Technology & Business Management at Masters’ Union. That experience pushed me toward product thinking and brought me to Aditya Birla Capital, where the work is tangible: real investors, real transactions, and real consequences when something breaks.

I’m a generalist by background. The problems I find most interesting sit at the edge of user needs, business outcomes and technical constraints.
